# Global Content Policy

The public platform is domain-neutral and intended for lawful, ethical community, commerce, service, support, automation, and content workflows.

User-facing defaults must remain lawful, respectful, and suitable for general commerce, services, support, community, automation, and content workflows. Domain-specific text belongs in configurable feature modules and should be reviewed before deployment.

Legacy compatibility identifiers may remain internally when changing them would break stored data or callable compatibility. They are not presented as public product positioning.

Python comments and docstrings in the public codebase are written in English.
